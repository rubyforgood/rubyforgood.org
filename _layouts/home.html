// our mission

Nonprofits run the world's most important software on the world's smallest budgets. We change that — one pull request at a time.

We're not a consultancy. We're not a job board. We're a community. Since 2013, thousands of volunteers — from first-time committers to staff engineers at the companies you've heard of — have spent weekends, weeknights, and one very intense long weekend each year shipping production software to mission-driven organizations.

Every line of code we write is open source. Every project we adopt lives on, maintained by the community long after the initial sprint. We measure our impact not in stars or downloads, but in foster youth advocates supported, diapers distributed, essentials banks coordinated, and stories preserved.

// how we work

Three roles. One shared standard: production-grade.

Everything we ship is meant to run for years — long after the last commit of the long weekend. That requires three kinds of people showing up.

01 / Builders

Developers who care about the user, not just the framework.

Ruby, Rails, JavaScript, mobile, dev-ops — every level from first PR to staff engineer. We pair-program, mentor in public, and ship in branches reviewed by humans.

Looking for:
· Rails contributors
· Frontend / Hotwire / React
· SRE & deploy mentors
02 / Designers

Designers who can ship interfaces a case-worker can actually use at 9pm.

Product design, UX research, accessibility, visual identity — nonprofits typically have none of this on staff. Your work shows up immediately.

Looking for:
· Product designers
· Researchers & A11y specialists
· Brand & illustration
03 / Organizers

Organizers who keep the humans humming.

Project managers, team leads, partner liaisons, mentors, event runners — the connective tissue that turns a weekend of caffeine into something that ships and survives.

Looking for:
· Team leads & PMs
· Nonprofit liaisons
· Mentors & newcomers' guides

// ruby for good 2026

One long weekend at a retreat center. Real projects, real people.

Programmers from all over the globe get together over a long weekend to build and contribute to open-source projects that help our communities. Shepherd's Spring Retreat Center, tucked into the woods of western Maryland.

  • All-inclusive: shared lodging, every meal, evening snacks, swag
  • Pair-program with newcomers and longtimers
  • Ship features that go live the Monday after
  • Real meals. Real campfires. No swag-table burnout.
