Ways to volunteer

Start with something small.

Build and test

Pick a project, read its contributor guide, and look for a good first issue. Testing a change or asking a clear question helps, too.

Browse projects on GitHub

Design and explain

Listen to nonprofit staff, sketch a screen, or write plain-language instructions. A feature only helps if someone can use it.

Introduce yourself by email

Connect and organize

Help a team plan its next release, share what a partner needs, or lend a hand with outreach and fundraising.

Volunteer with your team
